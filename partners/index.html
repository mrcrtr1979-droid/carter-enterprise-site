APPLY ONCE

How approval works

  1. Email the application below. We read each channel you list, as it is today.
  2. Approval is per property and is not automatic. We approve partners whose audience fits and who publish under their own name.
  3. If you are approved, you join that property's rail (the Namebeam portal or the Trunkline Gumroad form), and the rail issues your link.
  4. You receive the partner kit for that property: the product facts, the evidence pages, sample copy and the disclosure line.

Email your application

ATTRIBUTION AND PAYOUT

How referrals are counted and paid

  • Namebeam: a referral is credited by Endorsely when a visitor who arrived through your link pays through a Namebeam Stripe checkout within the window on the program page. Commissions are paid on the schedule on that page.
  • Trunkline: Gumroad credits the sale to your affiliate link under Gumroad's rules and pays you through your Gumroad account.
  • Only paid sales count. A refunded or disputed payment earns no commission.
  • Not every click or checkout can be credited, for example when a buyer blocks cookies or uses another device.
RULES

Disclosure is required

Every post, email or video that links to us must say plainly, near the link, that you earn a commission. For example: "I earn a commission if you buy through this link."

Not allowed

  • Promising rankings, traffic, leads, revenue, AI visibility or investment results.
  • Trading, investment, income or legal advice in Trunkline's name.
  • Earnings claims of any kind, to buyers or to other partners.
  • Numbers that are not shown on a Namebeam or Trunkline page, or that drop their dates and scope.
  • Self-referrals, spam, fake reviews or testimonials, or false scarcity.
  • Bidding on Carter Enterprise, Namebeam, Trunkline or Terry J Carter in paid search without written approval.
  • Making your own discount codes, or presenting yourself as our employee, official reseller or exclusive partner.
  • Promising custom deliverables, white-label files or delivery times.

We may decline an application, or suspend or end a partnership, for a breach of these rules.
