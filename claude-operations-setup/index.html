WHAT A PROCESS CAN BE

Examples of one process

One process means one repeatable job with a clear input and output. Examples: a weekly summary of new form submissions sent to one inbox; a daily check of a spreadsheet for rows that need action, with a written list; a first draft of a recurring report from files you name; a daily read of one mailbox folder that files each message under fixed labels. Anything that needs a password typed by us, a payment made by us, or a public post from your accounts is out of scope.
